Target rise, start drop, order and validity per judge

J10 · Giovanni's score: recall@k

For the selection above. Target at s = 0 / s = 1 = share judged as the target (J9: mean probability). Rise = s = 1 minus s = 0; start drop = start share at s = 0 minus at s = 1. Order = Spearman correlation between s and the position of the judged persona on its route (0 = start, 1 = target), over answers judged as a persona on the route (n on route). Brackets: 95% bootstrap interval, resampling questions. B → B: prompted target judged as the target. A → B: prompted start judged as the target. J3 and J4 label kinds of role, not personas, so they have no start or target. J6 names personas freely; its names are matched to the pair's personas only when they are spelled the same (its mapping to the 275 roles is not run yet), so its B → B is low. With 'J10 counts: any of its top 5', the J10 panel gives the share of answers with at least one of their first five ranked names in that group (each line between 0 and 100%); for the target this is recall@5. J10 is Giovanni Marraffini's ranking judge (branch persona-definition, make_rank_chunks.py / score_rank.py), ported exactly: it sees only the answer, whitespace collapsed and cut to 500 characters, 15 shuffled answers per request, and ranks 10 of the 276 Assistant Axis role names (default included). Its panel and the table above use its first-ranked name; recall@k is the share of answers whose target is among the first k names. Agreement between draws: answers judged in both draw 0 and draw 1 (n in brackets; Cohen's κ; each draw changes rephrasing, category order and examples at once).
