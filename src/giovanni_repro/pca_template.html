PCA is fitted per layer on the six prompted means (five personas and the empty prompt) and every setting's mean, so the three axes are the directions in which these points differ most; the share of their variance each axis carries is given above. All steered answers are re-read with the empty system block, while the diamonds come from answers read with their persona's system prompt, so a gap between a curve's end and its diamond is partly that context. Hidden state 23 is the one being steered; the page opens on the last layer (36), where the curves move clearly towards their personas. At layer 23 the steered answers stay about as far from the prompted means as the unsteered ones, because there the system prompt in the diamonds' context still dominates the reading.
