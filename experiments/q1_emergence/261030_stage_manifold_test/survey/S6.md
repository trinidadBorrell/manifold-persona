## 6. Points that contradict or change our plan

All "change" lines are inference unless a source is named.

**1. No precedent for a no-change claim.**
- Stated: Lakens says p > α does not show no effect. Kriegeskorte, Gröger §7 and Schütt §5.1.5 agree for their tests. None of the 25 offers an equivalence test or minimum detectable change on a representation measure. Three targeted searches found none. That is absence in these searches, not proof.
- Change: Fix a margin in raw units before the run. Plan for "undetermined" as an outcome.
- Size: Lakens Eq. 5 gives dz about .65 for 20 role pairs, and .46 for 40 question pairs. 800 cells would falsely give .10.
- Without theory, the margin is resource-based: it says only what we can reject.

**2. One run per stage limits the claim.**
- Stated: Sellam §1 and §3.2: a single artifact does not support a procedure claim.
- Change: Claim about these OLMo-2 checkpoints only, not about SFT, DPO or RL in general. Ding's seed baseline is not available for our stages.
- PolyPythias could give a proxy, but it covers pre-training and 410M at most.

**3. The obvious null is the wrong null.**
- Stated: Gröger §7 and C.1: grouped samples need restricted permutations. Josse and Kriegeskorte test association only.
- Change: Checkpoints reading one text are related, so a pairing null always rejects. Use it only as a chance floor. Never permute the 800 cells freely. Permute whole roles or questions, and add per-checkpoint generative nulls (T9).

**4. Rank moves scores on null data.**
- Stated: Gröger §4.1, Murphy Eq. 3, Josse Eq. 2.6.
- Check: at n = 800, d = 4096, raw CKA between independent clouds is about .84, and about .11 at effective width 100. The lead recomputed both as d/(d+n).
- Change: Our d/n of 5.12 is inside Gröger's range. Raw scores across checkpoints are not comparable when rank changes.
- **Unknown:** No paper shows that a flat-vs-curved held-out margin has this bias, or lacks it. We must simulate it per checkpoint.

**5. Unit RMS is not enough.**
- Stated: Kaufman: NMAPC removes only a global scale. Klabunde §5: rogue dimensions and normalization can hurt. Minder Fig. 1 footnote: chat activation norms shift the measure. Davari Thm 1: a participation-ratio term enters.
- Change: Keep unit RMS and the rank sweep. Add a preprocessing sweep and a covariance-matched surrogate per checkpoint. Curvature scales as 1/length, so a scale gain can pass as a curvature change.

**6. The bootstrap design sits at its limit.**
- Stated: Schütt §2.2.3: accurate at ≥ 20 subjects and ≥ 40 conditions, from RDM simulations. §5.1.4: df is the smaller N minus 1. §2.1.1: the naive form overstates variance.
- Change: Use the corrected form with roles and questions as factors, df 19. The limit may not hold for a GPLVM score. Refits may run into the thousands (§3.2), so cost needs a check. With no repeats, interaction and noise cannot be split.

**7. A curved win alone proves little.**
- Stated: Antognini §2.3: curved PCA shapes from a random walk. Cai: white noise reproduces most of the off-diagonal variance. Dujmović: test-set structure can create similarity.
- Change: Our cells have no time order, so the theorem does not apply directly. Roles picked along a gradient might still give an arch. Run the same GPLVM pipeline on noise and on a matched dependent null. `LITERATURE.md` puts the random-init floor out of scope (needs a GPU). At least three S6 papers use that control (Kaufman, Cui, Klabunde), so this needs a decision.

**8. The plan must allow non-monotonic change.**
- Stated: Razzhigaev §4.3, ID rises then falls, with no test. Saxe: feature curves can reverse. Li 2025: RankMe falls in RLVR.
- Change: Test all 10 checkpoints. Do not state the hypothesis as a steady rise.

**9. Fixed text may hide the change.**
- Stated: Minder §3.3: template tokens carry much of the change, 40% of latents mainly template. §3.2: KL reduction is larger on the first 9 tokens.
- Change: A template-free fixed text and mean pooling may both miss chat change. This supports the second text source. A null result would then not show "no change" in general (Lakens: equivalence holds for one operationalization).

**10. Do not use CKA as the "nothing changed" control.**
- Stated: Ding §3.2, Davari §4, Klabunde (none wins). Gröger: global and local similarity diverge.
- Change: Report at least one global and one local measure, with Procrustes as a candidate. Kornblith Thm 1 rules out CCA-type indexes at width 4096 > 800 cells unless we reduce first. Ding could not compute PWCCA when n < d.

**11. Input deconfounding is possible but carries its own assumption.**
- Stated: Cui regresses an input matrix from each similarity matrix. It needs linear, additive input effects (§3.1, §5), and text K0 is undefined (§4.4). Deep layers fail (§4.2).
- Change: The planned text floor could use this form. It addresses S11, not curvature. Crossed cells share roles and questions, so its noise assumption needs a recheck.

**12. Local curvature is out of reach, which supports the global plan.**
- Stated: Kaufman App. D and Fig. 3B.
- Change: 800 cells cannot give 1024 neighbours per point. Held-out global model comparison is the better fit here. Do not add a local curvature number.

**13. Supports for the plan.** Held-out scoring matches Diedrichsen and Schütt. Pairing across checkpoints matches Card and Sellam. Bootstrap over roles and questions matches Schütt. A planted-effect simulation before the run matches Card and Ding, and it would give the minimum detectable change we need.

**Limits.** No search proves completeness. English sources only. Two citation steps failed. Backward lists were not screened. S6 stopped at the paper limit, not at saturation.
