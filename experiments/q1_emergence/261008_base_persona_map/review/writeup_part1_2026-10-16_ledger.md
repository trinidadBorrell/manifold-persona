# Claim ledger for the 16/10 write-up (audit, E0, E3 Part 1)

Made 16/10/2026 with `/challenge-result`. It replaces the numbers in `claim_ledger_2026-10-16.md` and `ivan_update_2026-10-16.md`, which use the superseded `results/naming_sealed_v1.json`. Those two files are left as they are.

Every Part 1 number below comes from [results/naming_v2.json](../results/naming_v2.json) unless a row names another file. JSON paths are written as `key > key`. "GPT / Claude" means the two blind naming judges (GPT-5.6-Sol and Claude Sonnet 5.5). hit@5 = the target role is in the judge's top 5 of 276, macro average over 20 roles. Intervals are 95% crossed bootstrap (2,000 draws); p-values are block permutations (9,999). Mask `none` = every reply scored.

What changed from v1 to v2 (Codex review [codex_review_scripts_261016.md](codex_review_scripts_261016.md), blocker 1): the per-role null now uses the target role's own axis. Role coverage went up (ex2 GPT 14 to 17 roles), the wrong-role null is now per condition (2.2% to 3.9%, not one 2.4%), and some intervals moved (ex2 Claude 27.8-56.2 in v1, 29.7-55.9 in v2). No pass or fail decision changed.

Human-review status for every row: **none** (no independent human reviewer). An agent review is not independent human review.
