## T1 result: literature definitions of "privileged" (12 Nov)

Reader report (sources read in full; files in `output/lit_privileged_2026-11-12/`): no shared definition. Five in use: reference persona (Asvin G. 2026), lowest own-text surprise (G. & Lindsey 2026), preference leak into other turns (Nguyen 2026; Marks, Lindsey, Olah 2026), wider trait spread from the default (Zhao et al. 2026), default basin (Beckmann and Butlin 2026). Base-compatible without a chat template: Nguyen's plain-text coin-flip arm (about 400 fixed prompts, logits only), G. & Lindsey's no-template entropy arm (needs own generations and the LM head), Lu-style position on role PC1. Olmo 3 stage results already exist: own-text advantage appears at SFT for Assistant text and spreads at DPO (G. & Lindsey, OLMo-3-32B; checked in the saved text); the preference leak jumps at DPO (Nguyen, OLMo-3.1-32B).

New candidate task for the follow-up (owner to approve): T16, Nguyen's coin-flip test on OLMo-2-7B base (plain-text arm), SFT, DPO, RL steps and Instruct. Logits only, no generation, no judge; the cheapest stage test of "privileged". Needs Nguyen's prompt set and a matched control.
